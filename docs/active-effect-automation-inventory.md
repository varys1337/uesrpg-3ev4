## Notable findings

- Weakness to Fire, Frost, Shock and Poison each have two authored effects. `src/core/active-effects/weakness-compat.js` recognizes only the known spell identity and original negative template; its resistance representation is excluded from the modifier collectors. `src/core/active-effects/weakness.js` evaluates the typed and recognized legacy changes alongside active trait Items, choosing the highest value. Both authored effects are still created. A customized resistance change with a recognizable weakness spell identity is flagged for GM review in damage reports and retains its existing resistance behavior; an older effect without that identity cannot be distinguished reliably.
- `src/core/combat/damage/resolver/resolve.js` adds the highest matching weakness once per type after reduction, including when reductions reach zero, and excludes immune, absorbed and incorporeal-blocked components. `src/core/combat/damage/apply.js` handles the separate single-component path; `src/core/magic/damage-application.js` carries the pre-mitigation hit and suppresses repeated types in its separate component applications. These paths are not verified in Foundry. Weakness-related non-damaging tests, source-dependent types such as silver and sunlight, and whether an elemental spell also qualifies for a separate Magic weakness remain unresolved.
- Actor preparation now supplies actor, Item and effect context when resolving numeric effect templates. This enables dynamic expressions beyond weakness as well; their individual behavior has not been reviewed or verified in Foundry.
- Caster-side origin Active Effects remain in use for ordinary spells as well as upkeep, areas, summons and teardown. Target-owned cast grouping, discovery across scenes, authority, interrupted operations and all cleanup paths have not been implemented or reviewed completely. No casts or stored documents have been migrated.
- The remaining registry and runtime producer entries are search leads, not a completed audit. No claim is made that all supported keys, generated effects, transfer conditions, durations or cleanup paths have been verified.
